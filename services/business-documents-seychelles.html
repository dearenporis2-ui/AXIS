Clear, consistent information

A business document should help the reader understand what is being offered, what it costs, and what action comes next. A considered layout makes key information easier to find while giving your brand a consistent appearance.

AXIS can structure the document around the information your clients need and apply your visual identity across the agreed set of documents.

Documents can include

  • Proposals that outline a project and its scope.
  • Quotes that present services, quantities, and amounts.
  • Receipts or payment confirmations using your brand.
  • Reusable layouts for consistent future documents.

Designed for handoff

We agree on the document types, information fields, format, and editing needs before design begins. The final handoff depends on the agreed deliverable and can be discussed for PDF or editable workflows.

AXIS does not provide accounting or legal advice. Your financial figures, tax treatment, and contractual wording should be reviewed by the appropriate professional.

Useful for growing service brands

For a service business, a proposal or quote may be the first detailed impression a potential client receives. A consistent layout helps your team present its offer professionally, whether the document is sent after an enquiry or attached to a project discussion.

We can work from an existing logo and brand guide, or discuss a visual direction if your brand assets are still developing.

Questions about branded documents

What do I need to send?

Share your logo and brand colours if you have them, examples of your current documents, the information each document must contain, and the format you expect to use.

Can the documents be edited later?

That depends on the file format and workflow agreed for the project. Tell us who will update the documents and which tools they use so we can recommend a suitable handoff.

Can these be generated automatically?

Automated document generation can be scoped as a separate software feature. It is different from designing reusable document layouts and depends on your data and approval workflow.

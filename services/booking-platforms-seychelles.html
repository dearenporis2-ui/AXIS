Make the customer path clear

A useful booking or rental flow should answer practical questions early: what is available, what information is needed, and what happens after a request is sent. AXIS maps that journey with you before building the interface.

Depending on scope, a platform can include searchable listings, detail pages, enquiry or booking requests, and a clear confirmation step. Payment processing and availability rules need to be defined separately for each project.

Manage content in one place

AXIS has experience with Firebase-backed applications, Firestore data, image uploads, and authenticated admin tools. The specific setup is chosen to suit the features, expected usage, and maintenance needs of your platform.

Cloud infrastructure and image services may have their own usage limits or charges. These third-party costs should be checked against current provider plans as part of planning.

Good fit for changing catalogues

  • Holiday rentals and accommodation enquiries.
  • Equipment or vehicle rental listings.
  • Service catalogues with custom enquiry steps.
  • Businesses that need to update listings without editing code.

Start with requirements

Before development, define who can make a request, who confirms it, what data the business needs, and which actions require an admin login. These decisions help keep the first release useful and avoid building unnecessary complexity.

AXIS scopes each platform individually. Availability, customer accounts, online payment, notifications, and reporting are not assumed features; they are discussed and quoted according to your needs.

Questions about custom platforms

Is this the same as a booking plugin?

A custom platform is built around a defined workflow. It may suit a business with requirements that a standard plugin cannot meet, but a ready-made service can be more practical for simpler needs.

Are there ongoing costs?

There is no assumed monthly AXIS platform subscription. Hosting, Firebase, image storage, payment processing, or other third-party providers may charge separately based on the chosen setup and usage.

Can we add features later?

Future work can be scoped as the business changes. We document the initial functionality and access arrangements during handover.

Website support · Seychelles

Keep your website useful as your business changes.

AXIS provides practical website fixes, content updates, and new features for businesses that need help after launch. Support is scoped to the work required, with the timing and cost agreed before changes begin.

Ask about website supportSee selected work

Support that starts with the issue

Describe the problem or change, share the site address, and tell us what outcome you need. We can review the request, identify the work involved, and propose an appropriate next step.

Examples include correcting a display issue, updating service information, refreshing a project gallery, improving a contact flow, or discussing a new feature.

For existing AXIS builds and other sites

We can discuss support for websites built by AXIS as well as existing projects. For a site we did not build, the first step may be a review of the platform, code, hosting, and access arrangements.

Some projects may need a handover or technical assessment before changes can be estimated. We will explain that before starting work.

Agree scope before work begins

  • Describe the change or issue in plain language.
  • Share the URL, screenshots, and relevant details.
  • Confirm access, scope, and any dependencies.
  • Agree the quote and timing for the requested work.

Simple, transparent engagement

AXIS does not require a long-term support contract by default. Individual fixes or updates can be discussed as needed; ongoing arrangements can also be scoped if your business wants recurring help.

Hosting, domain renewals, paid plugins, and other provider services remain subject to their own terms and charges.

Questions about website maintenance

Can you help if I do not know what is wrong?

Yes. Share what you were doing when the issue appeared and what you expected to happen. We can discuss a review to identify the cause and possible fixes.

Do you offer emergency response?

Response timing is agreed for each request. Include any deadline when you get in touch so we can confirm whether it can be accommodated.

Can I request new features?

Yes. Larger changes are scoped as new development work, with the requirements and estimate agreed before implementation.
